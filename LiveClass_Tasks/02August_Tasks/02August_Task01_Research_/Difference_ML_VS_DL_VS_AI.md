# Machine Learning (ML) vs Deep Learning (DL) vs Artificial Intelligence (AI):

# Machine Learning (ML)
  Learning from data Example: Netflix recommends movies based on your viewing history.

# Deep Learning (DL) 
Building brain like neural networks Example: Google Photos groups pictures of your friends and family using facial recognition.

# Artificial Intelligence (AI) 
  Building real applications with ML/DL Example: Alexa or Google Home combine ML (speech recognition) and DL (intent understanding) into a usable product.

# Think of it like this:

	ML = the learning skill
	DL = the digital brain
    AI = the finished product that makes life easier

# Role Definitions:

Machine Learning Engineer (ML Engineer)
Teaches computers to learn patterns from data and make predictions.
Works on collecting, cleaning, and training datasets using algorithms.
Builds solutions like spam filters, fraud detection, recommendation engines, and sales forecasting.
Deep Learning Engineer (DL Engineer)
Designs and trains deep neural networks inspired by the human brain.
Tackles complex AI problems requiring advanced recognition.
Creates systems for image recognition, speech recognition, autonomous vehicles, medical imaging, and generative AI models.
AI Engineer
Focuses on building real world AI applications using foundation models (e.g., GPT, Gemini, Claude).
Integrates AI into products without training models from scratch.
Delivers solutions like chatbots, AI agents, document search, workflow automation, and intelligent assistants.

# In three top bullets:

ML Engineers → teach machines how to learn from data.
DL Engineers → build the digital brain for complex recognition tasks.
AI Engineers → use ML/DL to create real products people interact with.
